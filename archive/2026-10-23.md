### Biden-Appointed Judge Blocks DHS Noncitizen Voting Investigations in Nine States

A federal judge appointed by former President Joe Biden issued a preliminary injunction Wednesday, halting the Trump administration’s investigation into suspected noncitizen voting in nine states. The order, issued by U.S. District Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín, restricts DHS from conducting field operations targeting individuals who are known, suspected, or reasonably should be suspected of being U.S. citizens. The ruling applies to California, Connecticut, Georgia, Missouri, Nevada, New York, Pennsylvania, Washington, and Wisconsin, and remains in effect until Election Day on Nov. 3. The judge paused her order until 5 p.m. Friday to allow the administration time to appeal.

Martínez-Olguín ruled that the methods used by DHS to identify potential noncitizen voters, including state voter rolls, commercial databases, and AI tools, were unreliable and could lead to the wrongful identification of U.S. citizens. She found that the initiative likely violated a federal law prohibiting the intimidation of individuals who assist or encourage others to vote. The plaintiffs in the case include the California Nurses Association, National Nurses United, the Voto Latino Foundation, and local governments in San Francisco and Santa Clara County.

The Department of Homeland Security defended its methods, stating that agents use multiple sources to verify identities and eliminate false positives. A spokesperson said the agency is focused on identifying and removing noncitizens who vote, while noting it has no plans to investigate individuals believed to be U.S. citizens.

**Where the accounts differ**

The Daily Wire and FOX News both mention that the judge was born in Mexico and nominated by Biden, while The Guardian and CBS News refer to her as a Mexican-born judge. The Daily Wire and CBS News note that the judge worked for the American Civil Liberties Union, while FOX News explicitly states she worked for the ACLU.
