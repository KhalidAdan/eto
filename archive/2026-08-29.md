## Dolly Parton’s Legacy Under Fire as Left Claims Her as Socialist Mascot

Throughout her career, she consistently avoided becoming a political figure, rejecting attempts by media and politicians to label her as partisan. She declined the Presidential Medal of Freedom three times, once under President Trump and twice under Biden, to avoid misinterpretation of her political views. Despite her clear stance against politics, major left-wing figures quickly claimed her as a socialist after her death, despite her public record of nonpartisanship. ## Trump announces ‘biggest oil deal in world history’ with Venezuela

President Donald Trump announced a major oil deal with Venezuela, claiming it secures majority U.S. control of more than 65 billion barrels of proven oil reserves. The agreement, described as "the biggest oil deal in world history," is expected to lower gas prices for Americans. Trump stated the deal was negotiated by Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Secretary of War Pete Hegseth with Venezuela’s interim President Delcy Rodriguez and private businesses. Venezuela’s interim President Delcy Rodriguez welcomed the deal, stating it would "have a significant impact on our nation's revival." The agreement is expected to bring nearly $100 billion in private investment and over $209 billion in tax revenue to Venezuela.

The deal is part of Trump’s efforts to revive Venezuela’s oil industry, which has been in decline due to years of mismanagement and sanctions. Trump claimed the agreement would "substantially lower" gas prices for Americans "long into the future." The U.S. government would retain 55% control of a joint venture with an "experienced private operator in Venezuela," according to a U.S. official. The agreement also calls for the development of 17 strategic oil fields with a proven potential of 65 billion barrels.

## Tennessee national guard fatally shot armed man in back, autopsy shows

The Tennessee National Guard shot 20-year-old Tyrin Johnson in the back, according to an autopsy report obtained by the Associated Press. The report, shared by Johnson’s family, states a single bullet entered his right side and exited through his upper right chest. This contradicts initial police accounts that said Johnson was shot in the chest after turning toward the guards. Memphis police reported that Johnson was running from officers when he was shot, while the autopsy suggests he was running away from the shooter. The National Guard was part of the Memphis Safe Task Force, created by Donald Trump, and was involved in four fatal shootings between May and July.

Dr. Steven White, a forensic pathologist, said this is consistent with Johnson running away from the shooter. Johnson’s family attorney, Andre Wharton, stated that the law enforcement narrative does not match the autopsy findings, calling it a "blatant inconsistency."
