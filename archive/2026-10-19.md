### Macron says violent rioters will be ‘prosecuted and punished’ as buildings burn in France

French President Emmanuel Macron denounced violent rioters who attacked law enforcement and damaged national landmarks during escalating protests. The unrest, which began as student-led demonstrations over education policies, has expanded to include public sector workers and unions. The protests, which began last month at schools in Paris, have grown into a nationwide movement against inequality, weak public spending, and rising living costs. While most marches were peaceful, violent groups targeted public buildings, luxury boutiques, and supermarkets, attacking police vehicles and officers. The government has reported hundreds of arrests and injuries to police officers.

### GOP Senate candidate Mike Rogers breaks with Trump over Russia sanctions — as retiring lawmakers lash president
