### Trump waives Russia sanctions ordered by law named for Lindsey Graham

President Donald Trump signed an executive order on Friday waiving the sweeping Russian sanctions and tariffs enacted by the bill championed by the late Sen. Lindsey Graham. The move effectively neutralizes the law by lifting provisions that barred the president from removing sanctions against Russia and preventing a host of new sanctions and penalties from taking effect on Oct. 18, the deadline set by the law. The law, named after Graham, authorized tariffs of up to 100% on certain goods imported into the United States from major buyers of Russian oil and gas. Trump cited "national interests" as the rationale for the waiver, which he said would be in place until administration officials "determine that further action is appropriate."

The decision to suspend the sanctions came a week after Trump announced a deal to purchase diesel from Russia to try to lower global fuel prices, a move that drew criticism from both Republicans and Democrats. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy criticized the waiver, saying it would "fuel" Russia's war effort, while some lawmakers, including Sen.
