## Charter plane crash kills 8 near remote radar site in western Alaska, military says

A charter aircraft carrying eight people crashed at a remote radar site in western Alaska on Thursday, killing all on board, the US military said. The crash occurred near Cape Newenham Long Range Radar Site Airport, approximately 450 miles west of Anchorage, Alaska. The plane, a Cessna 441, had taken off from Anchorage and was believed to have crashed early Thursday afternoon on approach to Cape Newenham. The Federal Aviation Administration confirmed the crash happened about 12:15 p.m. Thursday west of Cape Newenham. The crash site is managed by the Pacific Air Forces Regional Support Center and is part of a network of remote radar sites that tracks aircraft operating in Alaska’s airspace and along its borders.
