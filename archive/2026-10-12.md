The IOM's Djibouti mission chief, Alexandre Coissac, described the tragedy as a "stark reminder" of the risks migrants face. The IOM also mentioned that the crossing from the Horn of Africa to the Arabian Peninsula is a common but dangerous route, with more than 900 migrants dying or going missing along the route last year.
