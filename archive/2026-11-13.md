### US doctor kills building manager after long-running dispute over mould, police say

A doctor who had been in an ongoing dispute over mould and other issues at his Washington DC apartment fatally shot his building's manager and fired at four others before killing himself, police say. Building manager James Stuart, 46, was pronounced dead at the scene, while four others were rushed to local hospitals for treatment. The shooting began on Thursday morning local time inside a gym in an apartment building in the Logan Circle neighbourhood, which is about a mile (1.6km) north of the White House. ### Microsoft Outage Disrupts Email, Workplace Apps
