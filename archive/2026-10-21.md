The WFP has also reduced food rations and hot meals, with deliveries now occurring every other day in October. Most families in Gaza still rely on aid due to high unemployment, with about 80% of the population unemployed, according to UN data.

Gaza’s agricultural and fishing sectors have been severely impacted, with farmland occupied by Israeli forces and fishing boats barred from coastal waters. A recent Unicef survey found that one in eight children in Gaza are chronically malnourished or stunted.
