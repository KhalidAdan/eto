He also questioned whether the conflict with Iran is pushing deployments far beyond planned durations. Acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth have not provided clear timelines for the ship's return to the U.S. and have disputed claims of misrepresented conditions.

Heavy rainfall in Chiba prefecture, east of Tokyo, led to at least four deaths and stranded thousands.
