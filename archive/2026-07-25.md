## At least one dead, several injured after van rams into crowd at Pride festival in Berlin.

A white van rammed into a crowd of people attending an LGBTQ+ event in Berlin's Tiergarten Park on Saturday evening, killing at least one person and injuring 16 others. The incident occurred during the annual Christopher Street Day festival, which was attended by hundreds of thousands of people. Police said they were searching for one or more suspects and had detained someone believed to be involved with the "Islamist scene in Berlin." The driver of the van is still at large.

The attack forced organizers to cancel the event, and police called on everyone to leave the premises immediately. Emergency services treated the injured, and some victims had life-threatening injuries. Berlin Mayor Kai Wegner described the incident as an "attack on our free and cosmopolitan society."

## India's Education Minister Resigns Amidst Nationwide Protests Over Exam Paper Leaks

Protests over exam paper leaks have led to the resignation of India's Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. The protests, which began in early June, were sparked by a series of leaks in nationwide exams for university seats or government jobs. The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP), a satirical group created on social media, has been leading the protests and demanding reforms to India's education system.

The CJP's founder, Abhijeet Dipke, announced Pradhan's resignation on Saturday, saying "We have done it." The protesters had been calling for Pradhan's resignation, as well as compensation for the families of students who died by suicide due to exam paper leaks. The protests have gained momentum across India, with thousands of people taking to the streets in cities such as New Delhi, Bengaluru, and Mumbai.

**Where the accounts differ**

Al Jazeera reports that the protesters are calling for a public apology from police and paramilitary forces for attacks on demonstrators, while NPR notes that the government had initially ignored the protesters before vilifying them.
