Gen Z men split from GOP in midterms, and broke hard for Democrats

The Hill highlighted that Gen Z men had been an important constituency for Trump in 2024, but in 2026, Democrats pulled them across the aisle. The Washington Examiner noted that Democrats took 50 percent of Gen Z men to the GOP’s 48 percent, marking a significant shift.

Where the accounts differ

The Hill and The Washington Examiner both report that Gen Z men shifted from Republicans to Democrats, but they differ in the exact percentages. Houston mayor curbs cooperation of US city’s police with ICE

Houston Mayor John Whitmire issued an executive order limiting the city’s police force cooperation with Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). Under the new policy, police will only contact ICE when a person is arrested for a jailable offense. The order was announced days after Democrats won all Texas statewide offices. The move aims to reduce fear and anxiety among residents, particularly immigrants.

The policy reverses a previous directive Whitmire had implemented in April under pressure from outgoing Republican Governor Greg Abbott. Abbott had threatened to withhold $110 million in state public safety grants if Houston did not comply with state law requiring cooperation with ICE. Whitmire stated he consulted Texas’s newly elected state leaders before acting, and that the change was intended to create a model for other cities.

Where the accounts differ

The Daily Wire and The Daily Caller do not mention this specific statistic. Former US senator and West Virginia governor Jay Rockefeller dies at 89

Jay Rockefeller, a former U.S. senator and governor of West Virginia, died at the age of 89. He was born into the prominent Rockefeller family and served in the Senate for nearly 30 years. Rockefeller was known for his advocacy of expanded healthcare and social policies, and for his criticism of the Iraq War. He was also a key supporter of President Bill Clinton’s failed health care initiative and President Barack Obama’s Affordable Care Act. His death was reported by multiple outlets, with his family confirming the news but not disclosing the cause.

Rockefeller was elected to the Senate in 1984 after serving as West Virginia’s secretary of state and two terms as governor. He chaired several Senate committees, including the Intelligence Committee, and led an investigation into the Bush administration’s pre-war intelligence assessments. He initially supported the Iraq War but later expressed regret, calling the decision based on "false premises." He retired from the Senate in 2015 and was remembered by colleagues and family members for his dedication to public service and his state.

New Jersey town reveals $26M theft days after election, sparking backlash

A New Jersey township, Cranbury, disclosed a fraudulent wire transfer of approximately $26 million on November 6, more than five weeks after the theft occurred. The funds were intended for purchasing property to meet the township's affordable housing obligations. Mayor Lisa Knierim, a Democrat, announced the theft, stating it was a "sophisticated financial theft" that took place on September 28. The township is working with a task force involving local, county, state, and federal law enforcement to recover the funds. So far, nearly $17.3 million in assets have been frozen, leaving about $8.7 million unaccounted for. The mayor acknowledged residents' concerns about the delayed disclosure and noted that the Township Committee plans to introduce new positions to strengthen financial oversight.

The disclosure came three days after the midterm elections, in which Democrat Thomas Szczur Jr. defeated Republican Carol Haring, securing Democratic control of all five seats on the Cranbury Township Committee. Residents expressed frustration over the timing of the announcement, with some demanding greater transparency and questioning the delay in disclosure.
